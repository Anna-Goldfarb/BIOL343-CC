# Background reading

Santangelo JS, Ness RW, Cohan B, Fitzpatrick CR, Innes SG, Koch S, et al. [287 authors] (2022) Global urban environmental change drives adaptation in white clover. *Science* 375(6586): 1275–1281. [10.1126/science.abk0989](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abk0989)

The Global Urban Evolution Project (GLUE) tested 110,019 white clover plants for HCN along urban–rural transects in 160 cities around the world.

**What to read:** the main text and figures; skip the Supplementary Materials.

Your dataset is simulated: this paper gives you the real-world context for your questions, but do not expect your effect sizes to match it. Cite the published version. The DOI link opens it through the Queen's Library when you are signed in.
